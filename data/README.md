# Local datasets

Place UTF-8 `.txt` datasets here. The trainer reads non-empty lines, drops exact duplicate lines, and applies a reproducible line-level train/validation split. The demonstration corpus in `demo_data/toy.txt` is deliberately tiny and only verifies the pipeline. Do not treat it as a useful language-model dataset. Keep private, copyrighted, personal, or unauthorized data out of Git. Files over 25 MB are rejected.
