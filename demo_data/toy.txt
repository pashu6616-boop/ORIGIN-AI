ORIGIN is a small language model experiment.
A Transformer learns to predict the next character from context.
This demonstration corpus only checks that the training pipeline works.
Toy text does not provide general knowledge or useful assistant abilities.
Training from random weights requires data, time, and computing resources.
Local documents can be searched separately to find matching excerpts.
ORIGIN keeps retrieved evidence separate from generated text.
The model uses causal attention to look only at earlier tokens.
Small experiments can run on a CPU with conservative settings.
